33-332.Z

Privacy Act Management

Ion DoD365 PII Reporter

Keep your eyes on the files in local, network, and cloud directories you can access. Ion checks them for Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and gives you a comprehensive report: an Excel workbook that summarizes and categorizes its findings. It only reads, and every value it finds is masked.

Key Facts

Universal:
Ion scans every type of file in the chosen directory.
Your access:
uses your CAC sign-on to scan, and saves nothing between sessions.
Read-only:
it makes no changes to anything it scans.
Data boundary:
nothing leaves DoD365; the export is saved to your computer.
Verifiable:
inspect the commented code yourself within a .txt before execution.
Accessible:
paste and run via ISE; no download, no admin rights, no policy change.

Have concerns? Speak with your WCO.

The steps to run it are at the top of the script.

How it works

Sign in→Read files in memory→Find PII→Mask it→Report
